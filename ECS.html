💻ECS: Experimental Computer Science🖱️

For ECS, we created 5 images on Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. The banner above is what I have been using for most of my websites. For the banner, we had to download a font, add some special effects to it, add color to the background, and then add a bit of 'pizzazz' around it.

For this image, we had to find a specific type of brush, and then start learning about how shading affects perspective as we drew.

For this image, we had to take an image of a pop star and then find a way to make the colors pop out by painting on a different layer.

For this image, we had to take a picture of a cartoon character, trace the outlines, and then color it in ourselves.

For this image, we had to take an image of a famous person and draw over them to make a portrait.
