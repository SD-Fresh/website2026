🖌️Art🖼️

For art, we had to create an album cover that represents us in some way. The first picture to the top left is my album cover.

The color in the sun surrounded by the clouds represents my ability to find beauty in nature, even the gloomy parts, and the stuffed animals watching the sun set represent my ability to take in the moment. I feel like the whole piece overall represents my calmness in general. I am pleased with it because I like the colors and shades I picked out, and it also looks unique while being mostly simple at the same time. I would probably pay more attention when drawing the swirls of the clouds if I did it again, because thanks to my lack of attention, it was difficult for me to find the right shade when deciding to fill them in or leave them white.
