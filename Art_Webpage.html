My Album Cover

We were tasked to work on a collaborative art piece, with 35 pieces.
I'm piece 4, and I had to communicate with the 3 pieces around mine
to ensure that our work and styles would match. You can find our
art piece in the hallway on the right, along with the two other group
pieces.

The goal for this assignment was to create a autobiographical album cover.
At first I had a lot of ideas, but I settled on the current one. It represents a
attempt or well a ode to freedom, which is because I think realizing yourfreedom, individuality, and will is important for a person. I'm not super pleased
with the drawing itself, and I forsure wish I worked on it earlier so I could have more time.
