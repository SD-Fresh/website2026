Recipe: Peanut Butter Banana Oat Bars

  • Take mashed bananas, peanut butter, and maple syrup. Mix well.
  • Add oats, flaxseed meal, and chia seeds to the mix! Stir until well and evenly combined. If desired, ingredients such as dried fruit or nuts may be added for flavor.
  • Put the mixture into an 8x8 baking dish lined with parchment paper. Make sure mixture is laid out and spread evenly. Refrigerate it for 3-4 hours to become firm.
  • When done, cut the mixture into squares, wrap them individually in plastic wrap, and enjoy! Keep leftovers refrigerated.
