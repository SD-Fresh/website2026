For this assignment, we were instructed
to make a atleast 6 second GIF
that has a background, subject
and movement. I chose to use
a fighter jet in the clouds
because of my admiration for
aviation and it fits the rubric given to us.

For this assignment, the instructions
said we were to use Google Slides to create
a presentation explaining the 7 principles of
design. Each slide should explain a concept,
and a graphic representation was to be shown.

For this assignment, we were to use
Adobe Photoshop to create a Digital
Landscape Painting. We were instructed
to base our painting off of a canyon
shown in the tutorial video. We also
utilized layers for each section of the
painting, such as the clouds or the ground.

For this assignment, we were told
to make a fictional tour poster based
on either a real or a fictional band.
A opening band was also required,
along with dates and locations.
For my band I chose I Promised The World
which is a real band that I like. For my opener,
I chose Skycamefalling, which is another real band.

For this assignment, we were to
choose a person that we wanted to
make a portrait of in Photoshop.
I chose Dan Hentschel, a actor
and comedian, who's known most
for his work on Youtube. I personally
enjoy his work and he's a great actor
so that's why I thought to choose him.

For this assignment, we were told
to find a person that we would make
digital art of. I personally chose a popular
Youtuber called Kreekcraft. I don't watch
him personally, but he's a influencer
and seeing how pop art is mostly made
from celebrities or influencers, I thought
he'd be a good fit.
